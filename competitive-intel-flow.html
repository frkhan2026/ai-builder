Sub-build details
1 Notion database + seed from Q1 doc 2–3 hrs
What it does
Creates the Notion database with the full schema. Seeds all 12 competitor pages with Q1 2026 historical data from the Word tracker as the baseline — so the database is immediately useful from day one, not empty.
Done when: every competitor has a populated Notion page with categorised entries. Database is browsable by competitor name and by Category filter.
2 Weekly trade publication scanner + Slack alerts 4–6 hrs
What it does
Scheduled agent runs every Monday. Scans 6 trade publications for any of the 12 tracked competitor names. For each hit: extracts headline, date, publication, and a one-sentence summary. Auto-assigns Category (Financial, Regulatory, Clinical Trial, etc.) and Significance (High / Medium / Low). Writes to Notion. Sends a Slack DM to Faisal for every High significance entry.

V2 / V3 features — ordered by what to add first
V2 — Add next
1 Sales team Slack channel alerts
Expand Faisal-only DM to a shared #competitive-intel channel. One-line change to Sub-build 2 — immediately useful to the full sales and marketing team.
2 ClinicalTrials.gov monitoring
Weekly scan for new trial registrations, enrollment updates, and completions per competitor. Catches IDE submissions before they hit the trade press.
3 LinkedIn company page monitoring
Scan competitor LinkedIn pages weekly for product launches, hiring surges, and conference posts. Competitors often announce here before issuing a press release.
4 Historical data seeding from past Word trackers
Load 3–4 prior quarterly trackers into Notion to build a longitudinal dataset. Prerequisite for trend analysis. Files available.
5 Quarterly trend summary
Auto-generated quarterly digest comparing competitor activity Q-over-Q. Replaces the manual Word tracker for the BOD meeting. Shows who is accelerating and what the key moves were.
V3 — Bigger lifts
6 KOL commentary log
Slack command /kol-note [competitor] [note] for capturing field intelligence from KOL conversations in real time — on a conference floor or after a call. Tagged by competitor, stored in Notion.
7 AKURA positioning layer
After each weekly scan, a second Claude pass maps top competitor moves to AKURA's differentiation points and flags threats or talking-point opportunities. Appended to Notion + included in Slack DM.
8 BOD report auto-generation
Quarterly auto-generation of a Word doc in the current tracker format, pre-populated from Notion. Ready for Faisal to annotate. Triggered manually 1 week before each BOD meeting.
9 Conference abstract monitoring
Auto-scan SCAI, PERT, TCT, and SIR abstract portals for competitor mentions when published annually. Complex due to site variability — needs site-specific handling per conference.
10 Published paper parsing
When the scanner finds a publication announcement, fetch and parse the paper to extract patient N, primary endpoints, and key results. Store structured data in Notion alongside the announcement entry.
Failure points & mitigations
Sub-build 2 — Scanner
1 · Silent zero results
Site changes or paywall blocks scraper — returns nothing with no error.
→ Slack DM if 0 entries written across all 6 pubs
2 · False positive matches
"Flow" or "Inari" matches unrelated content; junk written to Notion.
→ Require competitor name + domain qualifier
3 · Significance misclassification
Clearance tagged Medium; routine mention tagged High.
→ Include snippet in Slack DM for all High items
4 · Duplicate entries
Retry or multi-publication story written twice.
→ Deduplicate on Source URL before write
5 · Notion/Slack decoupled failure
One side fails silently while the other succeeds.
→ Write Notion first; log Slack failure as page comment
6 · Scheduler stops firing
Token expiry or task failure; tracker goes stale unnoticed.
→ "Last scan" timestamp in Notion + run-complete Slack DM
Sub-build 1 — Database
7 · Product synonym misses ⚡ highest leverage
Article says "Lightning Bolt" or "ENGULF" — not "Penumbra" or "E2."
→ Synonym dict: Penumbra → CAVT/Lightning Bolt/Flash; Inari → FlowTriever/PEERLESS/Artix; Imperative → Symphony; E2 → Hēlo/ENGULF; Jupiter → SPIRARE
8 · Schema drift → malformed writes
Someone renames a Notion property; scanner writes fail silently.
→ Validate schema at run start; skip + warn if property missing
Sub-build 3 — /intel-brief
9 · Stale data presented as current
Scanner hasn't run; /intel-brief returns old entries with no age indicator.
→ "Last scan: [date]" as first line; flag if >14 days old
10 · Notion pagination miss
API returns only first 100 entries; busy competitors silently truncated.
→ Paginate all queries via next_cursor; never assume first page is complete
